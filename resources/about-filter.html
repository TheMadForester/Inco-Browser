Inco Filter

A short host list Inco blocks before the page loads. It is not uBlock Origin and not EasyList.

What it does
  • Stops requests to known ad and tracker hostnames (and their subdomains).
  • Runs in the browser process. No extension API.
  • On by default. Turn it off in Settings.
What it does not
  • No cosmetic hiding (leftover empty ad boxes can remain).
  • No per-element rules, no community lists updating hourly.
  • Will not match uBlock on YouTube or heavy sites.

The list lives in the app as filter-hosts.txt. Growing it is how the filter gets better, not by installing someone else’s extension.
